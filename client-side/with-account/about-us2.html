How it started

From OJT experience to owning a business

Happy Queen's Crafty House was established in November 2025 by its owner, who turned a personal passion and hard-earned skills into a growing business. The idea of entering the printing industry began during the owner's on-the-job training (OJT) in college, in a company that offered printing services alongside computer shop operations. It gave valuable knowledge of the printing process and sparked a strong interest in the printing and creative industry.

Unlike the company where the owner trained, Happy Queen's Crafty House focuses on customized photo-based products and creative crafts, not just traditional paper printing. Every item combines creativity, craftsmanship and high-quality printing to fit what each customer needs.

Why we do it

Built on skill, passion, and creativity

According to the owner, the business was started to build something on the knowledge and skills already developed. Printing was where confidence and expertise were strongest, which made it the right foundation. But it is not only about printing, it is also about crafting, so customers can enjoy personalized and creatively designed products.

The owner's background in editing, graphic design and related creative fields added skills in digital design, layout and visual communication. Together with internship experience and prior knowledge of crafting, these became the foundation of a business that reflects both technical skill and artistic passion.
