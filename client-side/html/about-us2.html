About Happy Queen's Crafty House

The story behind the crafts, the prints, and the love we put into every order.

How It Started

From OJT Experience to Owning a Business

Happy Queen's Crafty House was established in November 2025 by its owner, who transformed a personal passion and acquired skills into a growing business. The inspiration to enter the printing industry began during the owner's on-the-job training (OJT) in college. During this period, the owner gained hands-on experience in a company that primarily offered printing services alongside computer shop operations, which provided valuable knowledge of the printing process and sparked a strong interest in pursuing a career in the printing and creative industry.

Unlike the company where the owner completed the internship, Happy Queen's Crafty House focuses on producing customized photo-based products and creative crafts rather than limiting its services to traditional paper printing. The business offers a variety of personalized items that combine creativity, craftsmanship, and high-quality printing to meet the unique needs of its customers.

Why We Do It

Built on Skill, Passion, and Creativity

According to the owner, the decision to establish the business was driven by a desire to start a venture based on existing knowledge and skills. Printing was the field where confidence and expertise had already been developed, making it the ideal foundation for building a business — but the owner also emphasized that it's not solely about printing, it's also about crafting, allowing customers to enjoy personalized and creatively designed products.

The owner's academic background further contributed to the development of the business. Having studied editing, graphic design, and related creative disciplines, the owner acquired skills in digital design, layout creation, and visual communication. Combined with practical internship experience and prior knowledge of crafting, these competencies became the foundation for a business that reflects both technical expertise and artistic passion.
