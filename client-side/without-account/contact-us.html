Quick answers

Before you message us

How do I place an order?

Pick a service in Our Services, customize it (size, design, text), and pay the down payment through GCash or PayMaya. You need an account to place an order, and it only takes a minute to register.

How does the down payment work?

A down payment starts your order, and you pay the balance when you pick it up or when it is delivered. You will see the exact amounts before you pay.

How does delivery work?

You can pick up at the shop, or have it delivered through Lalamove. The delivery fee is sent to you once your order is packed.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel while your order is still pending. If you already paid, the payment is refunded.

I'm not sure what I need.

No problem. Send us an inquiry or message us on Facebook with the product, size, quantity and deadline, and we will help you choose.

For the fastest reply, message us on Facebook. Tell us your product, size, quantity and deadline so we can prepare your quotation faster.
